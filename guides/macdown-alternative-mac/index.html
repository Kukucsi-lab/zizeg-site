The quick answer

If you like MacDown’s source-and-preview layout, try your existing files in Zizeg. Split keeps both panes visible, and both are editable. Your Markdown stays in its folder, with no library import.

Try the parts you use every day.

  • Edit the formatted document. MacDown shows a live preview beside source. In Zizeg, correct a sentence or table cell directly in Preview too.
  • Check your syntax fit. Zizeg currently doesn’t render math, Mermaid or footnotes. Math and Mermaid are planned for the next version, 1.2.0.
  • Switch when the file fits. Read a representative document for free before choosing Zizeg as your default.
