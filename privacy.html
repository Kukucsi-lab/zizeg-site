Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Zizeg is a Markdown editor for macOS that works on your Mac. The app does not collect your documents or track how you use it. This policy also explains the analytics used on the zizeg.app website.

The Mac app: no accounts or analytics

The Zizeg app has no user accounts, sign-in, analytics, or telemetry. It does not send your documents or information about how you use the app to us.

Website analytics

When you visit zizeg.app, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how the website is used and how its pages perform. The website loads an analytics script from Cloudflare and sends measurement data to Cloudflare when pages load. Its reports show measures such as page views, referring websites, country, browser and device type, and page-load performance. Cloudflare Web Analytics uses no cookies or local storage. We use these reports to understand overall website traffic, not to identify individual visitors. Website analytics do not access your Markdown files or measure your activity in the Zizeg app. Learn more about Cloudflare Web Analytics.

Your files stay on your Mac

Every document you open or edit in Zizeg stays exactly where you put it, on your own disk. Zizeg accesses only the files and folders you explicitly open or drag in, using macOS's standard sandboxed file-access system (security-scoped bookmarks) — the same permission model used by every sandboxed Mac app. We never see, receive, or have any way of accessing your documents.

Network activity in the Mac app

Zizeg blocks remote (internet-hosted) images in a document by default. If you choose to click and load one, your Mac makes a standard, direct connection to whichever server hosts that image — the same as opening a link in a browser. That server may log ordinary web-request information (such as your IP address), the same as any website or image host would; Zizeg doesn't add any tracking of its own to that request, and it never happens without you clicking to load the image first.

That's the only network activity the Zizeg app itself ever initiates. There is no other network access, no background communication, and no data sent to us at any point.

Purchases

If you purchase Zizeg through the Mac App Store, that transaction is handled entirely by Apple, under Apple's own privacy policy. We never receive your payment details.

Children's privacy

The Zizeg app does not collect data from anyone, including children. We do not use website analytics to identify individual visitors.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page and describe what changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@zizeg.app.
