Markdown, ready to send

Keep the Markdown.
Send a readable PDF.

The quick answer

Open your Markdown in Zizeg and choose File → Export → Export to PDF…. Pick the paper size and destination, choose any remote images to include, then check the finished PDF before sending.

A reading copy for the recipient.

  • Choose the paper, not the window. The PDF reflows to the selected page size. Wide tables scale to fit; check that their text remains readable.
  • Choose what travels. Include the remote images you need in the export picker. Front matter is excluded by default unless you select Include front matter.
  • Keep your working file. Export creates a separate copy. Open it to check page breaks, tables and images; your Markdown stays available for the next edit.
