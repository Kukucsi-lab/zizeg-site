Understand the opening lines

A block above the text.
Keep its purpose clear.

The quick answer

YAML front matter is metadata at the start of a Markdown file, often between --- lines. Zizeg shows it separately in Preview. Choose Edit in Source to change fields such as title, date or tags.

Metadata for tools. A document for readers.

  • Keep the fields you need. Other apps or publishing tools decide what the metadata means. A title field and the visible document heading are separate text.
  • Edit the opening block. Use Source for the fields, keeping their delimiters and indentation. Dashes elsewhere may be an ordinary divider, not metadata.
  • Choose the reader’s copy. Include front matter starts unchecked in PDF and HTML exports. Leaving it out of the export does not remove it from the Markdown.
