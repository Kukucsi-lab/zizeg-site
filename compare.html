Where Zizeg stands apart

Markdown control without giving up document editing.

Zizeg is designed for people who want Markdown to remain visible and trustworthy without making its syntax the only place where real editing happens.

  • Edit where you think: Preview and Source are equal editors.
  • Tune each view: choose the font family and size independently.
  • Use Mac document conventions: native tabs, autosave, and Finder files.
  • Keep files portable and safer: plain Markdown, local assets, and remote-image approval.

The closest technical alternative

Ferrite also makes both sides editable.

Ferrite is a free, open-source dual-pane Markdown editor whose raw and rendered views are both editable. That makes its core model closer to Zizeg than the Mac editors in the main table.

It appears separately because Ferrite is currently developed and tested primarily for Windows and Linux. Its macOS support is described as experimental, and its Mac builds are unsigned and not notarized.
